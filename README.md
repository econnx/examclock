# ExamClock

**Split your exam time by points and get a per-question countdown.**

→ **https://econnx.github.io/examclock/**

Most lost exam points aren't wrong answers — they're questions you never got to. ExamClock:

- takes the exam length, a buffer for checking, and each question's points;
- gives every question time proportional to its points and shows a "finish by" plan;
- runs a big countdown per question (turns orange at 1 minute, red + beep when it's time to move on), with a progress bar for the whole exam;
- has presets (25-point final, MCQ + essay, language test, 6 problems) and a shareable link to your plan.

One HTML file, no dependencies, works offline, nothing leaves your browser. Practise with it at home so the pacing is automatic on the day.
